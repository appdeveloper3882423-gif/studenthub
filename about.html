ABOUT STUDENTHUB

Everything students need in one place.

StudentHub is a free student-focused platform designed to make everyday academic, career and productivity tasks easier.

🎓

Built for Students

StudentHub brings useful calculators, study tools, productivity features, career resources and international utilities together in one simple platform.

🌍

For Students Worldwide

Whether you are in school, college, university or starting your professional journey, StudentHub is designed to be useful across different countries and study systems.

⚡

Fast & Simple

Most tools work directly in your browser so you can get things done without installing complicated software.

🔒

Privacy Friendly

Many productivity features use browser storage so your tasks, notes and preferences can remain available on your device.
