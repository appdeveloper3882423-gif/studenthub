Privacy Policy

This page explains how StudentHub handles information when you use the website.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

StudentHub is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection. Many features operate directly inside your browser.

2. Browser Storage

Some features may use browser local storage to remember information such as tasks, notes, habits, preferences and theme settings. This information normally remains on your device and is not automatically sent to StudentHub servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Some tools may communicate with external services to provide functionality such as QR-code generation or currency information. Those services may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

4. External Links

StudentHub may contain links to external websites or services. StudentHub is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Cookies

StudentHub may use browser technologies such as local storage for basic functionality and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

StudentHub is intended as a general educational and productivity resource. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from users through the basic website.

7. Security

We aim to provide a safe and reliable service, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when StudentHub introduces new features or services. Updated versions will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use the contact information provided with the StudentHub service.
